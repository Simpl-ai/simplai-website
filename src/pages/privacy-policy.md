# Privacy Policy

**Simplai Inc.**
Fort Saskatchewan, Alberta, Canada

**Effective Date:** June 6, 2025
**Last Updated:** June 6, 2025

---

## 1. Overview

Simplai Inc. ("Simplai", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the personal information of individuals who interact with our website at simplai.ca (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights under applicable privacy law.

We comply with the _Personal Information Protection Act_ (Alberta) ("PIPA"), which governs the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information by private-sector organizations in Alberta.

---

## 2. What Personal Information We Collect

We collect only the personal information you voluntarily provide to us through the contact form on our Site:

- **Full name**
- **Email address**
- **Company name**
- **Message** describing your current safety management needs or challenges
- **Newsletter/update subscription preference** (via opt-in checkbox)

We do not collect any sensitive personal information, financial data, or government-issued identifiers.

---

## 3. Why We Collect This Information

We collect this information to:

- Respond to your inquiry about our products or services
- Understand your organization's safety management context so we can respond appropriately
- Send you updates, product news, or newsletters, if you have opted in to receive them

The legal basis for collection under PIPA is your consent, which you provide by completing and submitting the contact form.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the purposes identified at the time of collection. We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to responding to your inquiry or sending communications you have opted into.

---

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers

To operate our Site and deliver communications, we use the following third-party service providers who may process your personal information on our behalf:

| Provider       | Purpose                            | Location      |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Web3Forms      | Contact form submission processing | Cloud-based   |
| Google (Gmail) | Email communication                | United States |
| Cloudflare     | Website hosting and delivery       | United States |

These providers are engaged to perform specific, limited functions and are not permitted to use your personal information for their own purposes. Where data is transferred outside of Canada, we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives comparable protection. Note that data processed in the United States may be subject to access by U.S. authorities under applicable U.S. law.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain personal information submitted through the contact form for a maximum of **90 days** from the date of submission, after which it is securely deleted. If you have opted in to receive newsletters or updates, we retain your name and email address for as long as you remain subscribed, or until you request deletion.

---

## 7. Data Security

We handle personal information in a secure manner consistent with industry practice. Our Site is hosted on Cloudflare's infrastructure, which provides encryption in transit (HTTPS) and related protections. Access to submitted information is limited to authorized personnel at Simplai Inc. who require it to respond to your inquiry.

We do not currently hold formal third-party security certifications (such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001), but we operate in a manner consistent with the security principles those frameworks represent.

---

## 8. Your Rights Under PIPA (Alberta)

As an individual whose personal information we hold, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you
- **Request correction** of any inaccurate or incomplete personal information
- **Withdraw consent** for the collection, use, or disclosure of your personal information (subject to legal or contractual restrictions)
- **Request deletion** of your personal information, where we are not legally required to retain it

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at [privacy@simplai.ca](mailto:privacy@simplai.ca). We will respond within 30 days, as required under PIPA.
