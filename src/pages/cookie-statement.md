## 2. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They are widely used to make websites function, to store user preferences, and to provide information to site owners about how visitors interact with their site.

Similar technologies include web beacons, pixel tags, and local storage, which serve comparable purposes.

---

## 3. Our Use of Cookies

**We do not use cookies on this Site for any tracking, analytics, advertising, or personalization purposes.**

Our Site is hosted on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Cloudflare may set certain technical cookies or use similar mechanisms as part of its network security, performance, and delivery services. These are operational in nature and are not under our direct control. They are not used by Simplai to identify or track individual users.

Examples of cookies Cloudflare may set include session-level identifiers used to manage traffic and protect against automated threats. These are typically short-lived and contain no personally identifiable information from Simplai's perspective.

For more information on Cloudflare's data practices, see [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Because we do not use cookies for tracking, analytics, or marketing, we do not present a cookie consent banner. If our cookie use changes in the future, we will update this statement and implement appropriate consent mechanisms.

## 5. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Instructions vary by browser:

- **Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox:** Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
- **Edge:** Settings > Cookies and site permissions

Note that blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of some websites.
