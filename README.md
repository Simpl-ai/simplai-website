## What will Simply Safe solve?

We want to hear from you — what are your biggest challenges with safety management?

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="60" align="center">🔔</td>
    <td><b>Arrival & Alerting</b></td>
    <td>Are daily hazard assessments (FLHAs) difficult to manage? Is it hard to alert workers of dangers before their shift begins?</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="center">☁️</td>
    <td><b>Incident Management</b></td>
    <td>Are your workers able to report incidents quickly? Can you track them effectively?</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="center">📋</td>
    <td><b>Corrective Actions</b></td>
    <td>How are you tracking corrective actions? Paper forms and pens? Are they being completed on time?</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="center">📡</td>
    <td><b>Emergency Broadcasting</b></td>
    <td>In an emergency, how quickly can you alert everyone on site? Do you have a reliable system to send urgent messages?</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="center">🛡️</td>
    <td><b>COR Compliance</b></td>
    <td>Is staying in compliance with COR standards taking too much time? Are you spending hours on paperwork?</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td align="center">💬</td>
    <td><b>Claims</b></td>
    <td>Do you struggle to get claims approved? Do you spend hours finding the correct information for valid claims?</td>
  </tr>
</table>

## About Us

We at Simpl AI understand the challenges you face because we've been there ourselves. We've sat in the meetings where projects drift off course, watched teams wrestle with tools that create more work than they save, and felt the frustration of solutions that look great in a demo but fall apart in the field.

### Our Values

> Technology should serve people, not the other way around.  
> That belief shapes everything we do.

We lead with transparency and honesty — no hidden agendas, no overpromising, no jargon meant to confuse rather than clarify. We believe the best solutions are the simple ones, and we relentlessly hunt down complexity so our customers don't have to. And we never stop learning. Every project teaches us something new, and we carry those lessons forward to the next client, the next challenge, the next opportunity to do better.

### Our Mission

> We started Simpl AI to make automation practical and accessible for real businesses — not just the ones with dedicated IT departments and enterprise budgets.

We know that saving a team a few hours each week compounds into something meaningful: more time for the work that matters, fewer late nights catching up on admin, and people who actually enjoy using the tools they're given. We don't build software and then look for problems to solve. We find the friction in your workflow and craft solutions that fit the way you already work.

### Our Experience

> Our team brings together years of hands-on work across project management, client relations, software development, quality assurance, process analysis, automation, efficiency, design, and problem solving.

We've delivered successful projects in healthcare, finance, and retail. We've worked with startups finding their footing and enterprises looking to modernize. What ties it all together is a stubborn insistence on making things better — simpler, faster, more reliable, more human. We've done this before, and we bring every lesson learned to the table for you.
